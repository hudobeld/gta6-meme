The meme: after 13 years of waiting for GTA 6, the make.fun ghost stops waiting and builds games for real money instead. Vice City neon street, the make.fun ghost front and centre.

The post itself is the entry — [view it on X](https://x.com/JamesMilleq2z/status/2124250028843917507). `POST.json` names it, and `main.ts` (Node 22, standard library only) copies `POST.json` to `/output` for the gate.

make.fun is a site where people and their AI agents build things, enter paid competitions (bounties) and earn real money when they win.
